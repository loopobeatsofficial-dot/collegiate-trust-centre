Privacy Policy

Collegiate helps Canadian students aged 16 and over plan applications, costs, funding and campus life. This policy explains exactly what leaves your phone, who receives it, and your choices.

Effective October 1, 2026 · Operator: IronWolfProductions, Canada · Contact: Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your writing, Funding Profile answers, saved schools, cost plan and planner are stored on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email, an encrypted password, an optional preferred name and your 16+ confirmation.
  • AI writing feedback is off unless you turn it on, and only sends the draft you choose.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Documents and saved versions, your experience ledger, Funding Profile answers (including optional answers about Indigenous identity, disability and family income), saved schools and programs, your cost plan, planner items, imported timetables and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device, separately for each account. They are not sent to us or anyone else, except when you use one of the features in sections 4 and 5 or share an export yourself.

2. Account information (optional)

If you create an account we store: your email address; your password, stored by our authentication service only as an irreversible hash; an optional preferred name; your confirmation that you are 16 or older; and the dates your account was created and updated. Our authentication service also keeps security records of sign-in activity, such as the time, IP address and device type, to protect accounts from misuse. We use account information only to sign you in, keep your account secure and send you sign-in codes.

3. Sign-in emails

Confirmation, sign-in and password-reset codes are sent by our email provider from no-reply@sovereign-me.com. The provider receives your email address and the message we send. We don't send marketing email.

4. AI writing feedback (only if you turn it on)

AI feedback is off until you agree on a consent screen that names the provider. It runs only when you tap “Get feedback” on a specific draft. We then send only that draft's text, its application prompt, its document type and its word or character limit, through our own server, to OpenAI in the United States. We never send your name, email, account ID, schools or Funding Profile answers. Your text is sent as written, so leave out anything personal you don't want shared.

Our server does not store your draft or the feedback. It keeps a count of how many feedback requests your account made each day, to enforce a daily limit. Under OpenAI's published API terms, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. You can turn AI feedback off at any time in Settings.

If something you write suggests you may be in crisis, the app shows free support lines (9-8-8 and Kids Help Phone). The AI is not a counsellor and does not contact anyone.

5. Textbook search and links

When you search for a textbook, your search words are sent from your phone directly to Open Library (Internet Archive) to look up editions. As with any web request, Open Library sees your IP address; we don't send any account information. Links to schools, award providers and support services open in an in-app browser, and those websites' own privacy policies apply. Tapping a phone number opens your phone app.

7. What we don't do

No advertising, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no access to your location, contacts or photos.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use Collegiate without an account: choose “Explore without an account” on the first screen.
  • Export your data: Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of everything stored in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account permanently deletes your account, profile and AI usage count from our servers, then clears the app's data on your phone. Copies held under a provider's own retention rules (such as OpenAI's 30-day abuse-monitoring logs and email-delivery logs) expire on their own schedules.
  • Turn off AI feedback at any time in Settings.
  • Access, correction and questions: email us. We reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca), Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca), or the commissioner in Alberta or British Columbia.

9. Keeping information

We keep account information until you delete your account. Information on your phone stays until you delete it, delete your account or remove the app.

10. Age

Collegiate is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date here, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. The person responsible for protecting personal information at IronWolfProductions can be reached at Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com.
