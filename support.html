Support

Questions, problems or privacy requests about Collegiate. We're a small team and reply by email, usually within a few business days.

Contact

Email Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com with “Collegiate support” in the subject. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the screen you were on, and what you expected to happen.

Never send passwords or sign-in codes. We will never ask for them, or for your essays or Funding Profile answers.

Common answers

  • Didn't get a code? Check spam for an email from no-reply@sovereign-me.com, then tap “Resend code”. Codes expire after 15 minutes. If you already have an account, sign in or reset your password instead.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and enter the code we email you.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. It's immediate and can't be undone.
  • Get a copy of your data: Settings → Export my data.
  • Turn AI feedback on or off: Settings → AI writing feedback.
  • Wrong or outdated school information? Email us the page and what's wrong. Always confirm details on the school's official site before you apply or pay.

Need to talk to someone now?

Call or text 9-8-8 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) or call Kids Help Phone at 1-800-668-6868; both are free, confidential and open 24/7. In an emergency, call 9-1-1.
